About This Archive

This comprehensive archive contains information about every known Grateful Dead concert from their formation in 1965 until their final show in 1995. The data includes venue information, setlists, and historical context for each performance.

The Grateful Dead were known for their improvisational style, unique setlists, and dedicated fanbase. Each show was a unique experience, making this archive a treasure trove for fans and music historians alike.
